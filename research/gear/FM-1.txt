Glob

Seq
1. Add recording of long notes. In the current step, hold down the desired note and rotate 'select' to select the target step. Release the button and the long note recording will not overwrite the previous note

Edit

3. New timbre copying: On the second page of the globe sub page of the Edit interface, you can select other timbres to overwrite the current timbre. Turn K3 to the right to enter, and turn K3 to the left in the selection interface to exit. Rotate K4 to select the timbre to be copied, and rotate select to confirm
